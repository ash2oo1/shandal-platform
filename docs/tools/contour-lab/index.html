2D Contour Mapping Lab

Publication-ready contour maps from your point data.

Load X, Y, Z points from a total station, GPS, boreholes or any spatial variable, interpolate them by ordinary kriging or inverse distance, and lay out a print-ready contour map with variogram and cross-validation.

Overview

The kriging variogram is fitted automatically (spherical model, weighted least squares) and every point is predicted from the others to show how reliable the surface is. The map can be clipped to the data hull so nothing is extrapolated.

What you enter · What you get

What you enter

  • CSV, TXT or XYZ file, or pasted rows
  • Projected or geographic coordinates
  • Method, grid size, interval and map style

What you get

  • Contour map with grid, scale, north arrow and legend
  • Experimental and fitted semivariogram
  • Leave-one-out RMSE and R²

Method and equations

Ordinary kriging

Best linear unbiased estimate with a fitted spherical variogram.

γ(h) = c0 + c [1.5 h/a − 0.5 (h/a)³], h ≤ a

Cross-validation

Dubrule’s (1983) closed form gives every leave-one-out error from one matrix inverse.

ei = wi / (A−1)ii

  3. Shepard, D. (1968). A two-dimensional interpolation function for irregularly-spaced data. Proc. ACM National Conference, 517–524.
